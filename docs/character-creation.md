# Character creation and advancement

Open **Create Character** in the Actors directory for a new Shinobi, or **Creation & Advancement** on an owned PC sheet. Existing characters retain their attributes, Items, and XP; the purchase tabs use their current totals.

The Tables & Identity tab supports the Playtest's Profession table (PDF p.17), seven rolls of 2d10+35 with unique assignments and the Profession's +5 adjustment (p.19), appearance tables (p.23), Chakra Nature (p.29), and War/Peace alignments (p.30). Each table can also be chosen manually. Results and drafts are saved on the Actor. The book prints overlapping alignment ranges at 96; the first printed matching row is used.

Initialization grants 1,000 XP, buys the Specialist Trait for 100 XP, and adds the Profession and selected Heritage. It also grants any equipment explicitly configured on the Profession Item. Starting-equipment choices printed in the Profession's description still need to be selected in Inventory. Heritage bonus choices are edited on the Heritage Item using the button in the Profession Advances tab.

Profession purchases cost 100 XP each. A Skill may advance once per Profession and cannot exceed three learned ranks. Bonus Advances increase the corresponding Primary Attribute Bonus, not the attribute percentage. Talent purchases add the linked Item. Profession slots are marked completed and purchases are recorded in the XP ledger.

Technique rank prices are E 100, D 200, C 300 (PDF pp.72–75), B 400, A 500, and S 600 XP. Matching Chakra Nature halves learning cost (p.29). An explicit **Learning XP cost** on an Item replaces its rank price. Draft Techniques cannot be learned until they are configured. Techniques already known and the Intelligence Bonus +3 limit are checked before purchase. The list defaults to affordable, learnable Techniques.

Undiscovered Nature adds 500 XP usable only for Techniques. The ledger tracks this restricted balance separately and spends it first on Techniques. Other advances can spend only General XP. Unspent starting XP is banked when creation is finished and can be spent later through the same tool, along with later XP awards. The GM can award XP from the ledger tab.

# Conditional bonuses

Add named situational modifiers under **Conditional Bonuses** on Skills & Combat. Choose all tests, an attribute's tests, one skill, a Primary Attribute Bonus, a secondary statistic, or flat damage, then enter a signed value and toggle it when the situation applies. The same toggles are available in the roll dialog and update its displayed chance. They persist until toggled off. Permanent passive modifiers remain Active Effects.

Use **Refresh Choices & XP** after adding catalogue Items or changing learning prices outside the tool.

Conditional percentages participate in the existing skill subtotal cap. NPC printed-chance overrides also receive applicable conditional percentages. No Active Effects are created or removed by conditional toggles.
